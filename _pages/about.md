I am an **AI Research Engineer** currently based in **Tokyo, Japan**, working at the intersection of **Aerospace Engineering** and **Geometric Deep Learning**.

My research focuses on **AI for Science**: designing neural architectures that respect physical laws (symmetries, conservation constraints) while scaling to industrial complexity. I am particularly interested in **State Space Models (SSMs)**, **Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)**, and **Differentiable Physics** using JAX.

Currently, I work at **IHI Corporation** in the Corporate R&D Division (Technology Planning). There, I lead the digital transformation of techno-economic modeling pipelines and develop time-series forecasting frameworks for complex combustion experiments.

Previously, I was a Visiting Researcher at **EPFL (Switzerland)** in the [Intelligent Maintenance and Operating Systems Lab](https://imos.epfl.ch/), working with **Prof. Olga Fink**. At EPFL, I developed Physics-Informed GNNs for damage localization in composite structures, implementing techniques from DeepMind's Graph Network Simulator.

I hold a Dual Degree (B.Tech + M.Tech) in **Aerospace Engineering with a Specialisation in AI** from **IIT Kharagpur**, graduating with Honors.
