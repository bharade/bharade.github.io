I am an **AI Engineer** based in **Tokyo, Japan**. Currently, I work at **IHI Corporation** in the Corporate R&D Division (Technology Planning Department). I am spearheading Physical AI development and digital transformation of techno-economic modeling pipelines at [IHI Corporation](https://www.ihi.co.jp/en/), one of Japan’s largest and most influential heavy-industry conglomerates.

My research focuses on **AI for Science**: designing neural architectures that respect physical laws (symmetries, conservation constraints) while scaling to industrial complexity. I am particularly interested in **building AI powered physical sytems**, **Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)**, and **Industrial AI**.

Previously, I was a Research Assistant at **EPFL (Switzerland)** in the [Intelligent Maintenance and Operating Systems Lab](https://imos.epfl.ch/), working with **Prof. Olga Fink**. At EPFL, I developed Physics-Informed GNNs for damage localization for industrial systems, implementing techniques from DeepMind's Graph Network Simulator. 

I hold a Dual Degree (B.Tech + M.Tech) in **Aerospace Engineering with a Specialisation in AI** from **IIT Kharagpur**, graduating with Honors.
